About
Hi, I'm Aditya.

I'm currently pursuing my MS in Robotics at Northeastern University. My work right now involves multi-robot coordination, vision-based manipulation, and ROS2-based simulation — including a thermal controls project for the ISS in collaboration with JAXA.

I did my undergrad in Mechanical Engineering at NIT Rourkela and then worked for about three years as a Project Engineer at Thermax, where I dealt with thermal systems, FEA, and manufacturing coordination. That background in simulation and hardware is something I carry into my robotics work — it helps when you need to think about how a robot actually holds up physically, not just how the software runs.
